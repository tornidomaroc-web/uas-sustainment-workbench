# Field census: method

`census.md` and `census.json` are generated by `uasw-census run`. They hold counts only.

## Samples (2026-09-25)

**PX4, Flight Review public logs.** Index snapshot of 469,733 public logs. Eligible: fixed-wing
or VTOL (standard, tiltrotor, tailsitter), not simulated (`sys_hw` without `SITL`), at least
120 s long, uploaded 2023-01-01 or later. That leaves 13,041 fixed-wing logs (1,932 distinct
aircraft ids) and 18,509 VTOL logs (2,010 ids); logs with no id count as one aircraft each,
giving 1,984 and 2,029 sampling units. The sample shuffles *aircraft* with seed
20260925 and takes one random log from each: 20 fixed-wing and 20 VTOL aircraft. No
candidate was over the 300 MB cap, so none was replaced.

Why not the official script's own filters alone: `download_logs.py` sorts newest first, so
`--mav-type "Fixed Wing" --max-num 20` returned 20 logs from 6 aircraft, all uploaded on the
same day. The script is still what downloads the logs (its `--log-id` filter, 6 s delay).

Why 40 and not 20: the open question is a proportion (how many logs carry a field). With 20
logs split over two airframe classes, one class would rest on 10 logs; with 20 per class the
95 % interval is about ±22 points at worst, enough to tell "almost always" from "rarely",
which is the decision the counter design needs.

**Whole-index rows.** Flight Review computes `vehicle_uuid` (from the log's `sys_uuid`) and
the count of ERROR-level messages at upload, so those two are measured on every eligible
log, not only the sample.

**ArduPilot, CMU ALFA.** `dataflash.zip` contains ten `.bin` logs; all ten are read. They
come from one aircraft on two days in July 2018. This is a census of the whole ALFA
DataFlash population, but it is **not** a sample of ArduPilot fleets.

## Definitions

- *Logs with field*: the field is defined and has at least one sample.
- *Logs with real values*: at least one sample meets the test in the "Real value means" column
  (for most fields: finite and non-zero; for state fields: the state actually changed).
- *Airborne*: PX4 `takeoff_time` set or `landed` false at some point; ArduPilot
  `STAT.isFlying` true, or GPS ground speed above 5 m/s.
- *Derivable counter*: every field the counter needs has real values in that log.
